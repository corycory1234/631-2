03  —  About / Philosophy

Photography is the
art of observation.

I believe the most compelling images are found, not made. My practice centers on patience — waiting for light, for honesty, for the moment before it becomes a moment.

Working across documentary, editorial, and commercial photography since 2014, I've collaborated with magazines, brands, and individuals who value stillness and craft.

04  —  Approach

How I Work

01

Observation & Consultation

Every project begins with listening. Understanding your vision, the story you need to tell, and the silence around it.

02

The Shoot

On location, I work with minimal interference. Natural light where possible. Patience always. The frame reveals itself.

03

Curation & Editing

From hundreds of frames, a handful earn their place. Each final image is graded with restraint — color serves truth, not trend.
